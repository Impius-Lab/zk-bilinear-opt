## 3. Technical Architecture & Methodology

Our discovery-and-verification stack enforces four machine-checked guardrails that eliminate AI mathematical hallucinations:
1. **Zero-Shared-Code `R1` Re-Derivation**: Every candidate theorem, Lean 4 formalization, `.axiom` PCC schedule, or bilinear identity is independently re-implemented from raw definitions by a separate reviewer agent before acceptance.
2. **Adversarial Live Mutation & Soundness Gates (`R2` + `--selftest`)**: Every verification script, Lean 4 proof, and circuit branch undergoes live fail-first witness-tampering and mutation testing (catching subtle multi-region `min`-crease assumptions such as `Mutant M19`, `GF(2)` bitmasks, Karabina $`z_2 = 0`$ branches, and `log2` outward-rounding directions), asserting **100% RED detection**.
3. **Proof-Carrying Code (`AXIOM`) + Kernel-Checked Lean 4 + Outward-Rounded Interval Certifiers**: Optimization schedules carry Z3 + `< 2,000`-LoC `axiom-tcb` LF proof certificates, algebraic identities compile in Core Lean 4 (`#print axioms`), and non-convex entropy certificates are verified across millions of rational parameters via `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` 128-bit (`Q15.112`) outward-rounded Safe-Rust and zero-dependency Python integer intervals.
4. **Symmetry Compression & Pareto Circuit Synthesis**: Equivariant dimensional folding (`6 -> 1` $`S_3`$ compression + exact RREF marginal-rigidity elimination) brings supercomputer-class tensor optimization (`CW_5^{\otimes 8}` and fiber-tied `CW_5^{\otimes 16}`) onto single-GPU workstations, while Pareto circuit synthesis optimizes $`(r, \Vert U\Vert_1 \Vert V\Vert_1, \Vert W_{\mathbb{Z}}\Vert_1 + D)`$ directly inside production ZK compilers.
