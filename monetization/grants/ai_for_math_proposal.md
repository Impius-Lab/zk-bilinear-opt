## 3. Technical Architecture & Methodology

Our discovery-and-verification stack enforces four machine-checked guardrails that eliminate AI mathematical hallucinations:
1. **Zero-Shared-Code `R1` Re-Derivation**: Every candidate theorem, Lean 4 formalization, or bilinear schedule is independently re-implemented from raw definitions by a separate reviewer agent before acceptance.
2. **Adversarial Live Mutation & Soundness Gates (`R2` + `--selftest`)**: Every verification script, Lean 4 proof, and circuit branch undergoes live fail-first witness-tampering and mutation testing (corrupting signs, `GF(2)` bitmasks, Karabina $`z_2 = 0`$ branches, quotient top-limb bits `1 << topWidth`, and CRT bounds), asserting **100% RED detection**.
3. **Kernel-Checked Lean 4 + Exact Symbolic Certificates (Zero Floating-Point Trust)**: Identities over $`\mathbb{Z}`$, $`\mathbb{Q}`$, $`\mathbb{C}`$, $`\mathbb{F}_p`$, and $`\mathbb{F}_{2^k}`$ are certified in Core Lean 4 (`#print axioms`) alongside fraction-free Bareiss determinants, Rabin irreducibility, Macaulay matrices, Gröbner Krull codimensions, and rational Kantorovich bounds.
4. **Compiler-Verified Pareto Synthesis**: Candidate schedules are optimized jointly across $`(r, \Vert U\Vert_1 \Vert V\Vert_1, \Vert W_{\mathbb{Z}}\Vert_1 + D)`$ and verified directly inside production compilers (`gnark`, `Plonky3`, `stwo`, `Scarb Cairo`, `snarkVM`, and `arkworks`).
